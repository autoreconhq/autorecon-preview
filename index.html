ABOUT US

Welcome to Auto Recon, LLC

Restore. Protect. Preserve.

Founded in 2018 by Walter Ross, Auto Recon, LLC has evolved from humble beginnings into a high-end auto detailing service built on precision, pride, and results. From daily drivers to luxury vehicles, every car we touch is treated with one goal in mind—bringing it back to a true showroom finish.

W

Walter Ross

Founder & Lead Detailer

Walter is more than a detailer—he’s the foundation of Auto Recon and the standard behind every result we deliver. A true perfectionist, he has an unmatched eye for detail and a deep-rooted passion for the craft. He doesn’t just clean vehicles—he restores them, elevates them, and refuses to settle for anything less than flawless. His journey hasn’t been easy, but his resilience, experience, and relentless drive have shaped the level of quality and consistency our customers have come to expect. What sets Walter apart isn’t just skill—it’s the pride he takes in every vehicle as if it were his own.

T

Tara

Co-Owner & Operations

Co-owner Tara is the driving force behind the operation—designing, building, and controlling the systems that power the business. She leads marketing, technology, operations, booking infrastructure, and web development with a hands-on, high-level approach that keeps everything running efficiently and ahead of the curve. She’s not just behind the scenes—she’s actively shaping the growth of the brand and isn’t afraid to step in on the physical side as well, including paint protection film (PPF) installations when needed.

C

Chris

Co-Owner & Protection Specialist

Chris, co-owner, U.S. Marine veteran, and Fluid Film specialist, plays a key role in expanding our protection services. He focuses on undercoating and long-term vehicle preservation, helping customers protect their investments from rust and harsh conditions. The discipline and attention to detail he developed as a Marine carry directly into his work at Auto Recon. As he continues to develop his detailing skills alongside Walter, he brings both dedication and versatility to the team—strengthening the foundation for our future growth.

Now operating from our shop in Middletown, this marks a major step forward for our team. Our vision is to build, train, and scale—developing top-tier talent, opening multiple locations, and continuing to raise the standard in the industry.

At Auto Recon, we don’t just clean vehicles—we transform them. We restore, protect, and preserve—delivering long-term results with protection options ranging from 1 to 10 years.

This is more than detailing.

This is Auto Recon.
