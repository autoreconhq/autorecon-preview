ABOUT US

Welcome to Auto Recon, LLC

Restore. Protect. Preserve.

Founded in 2018 by Walter Ross, Auto Recon, LLC has grown from humble beginnings into a premier auto detailing service dedicated to making every vehicle look and feel brand new. We’ve worked on everything from entry-level rides to upscale luxury vehicles, always prioritizing precision craftsmanship, exceptional customer service, and complete client satisfaction above all else.

Walter, a true perfectionist, leads with an unmatched eye for detail and a relentless commitment to excellence—consistently delivering results that bring vehicles back to showroom condition.

Alongside him is co-owner Tara, the strategic engine behind the brand. She drives the business forward through high-level marketing, technology, operations, booking systems, and website development—building and optimizing everything that keeps Auto Recon running at full speed. Her versatility extends beyond the digital space, as she also steps in for hands-on work, including paint protection film (PPF) installations when needed.

Chris, co-owner and Fluid Film specialist, plays a key role in expanding our protective services while continuing to sharpen his detailing expertise under Walter’s guidance.

After two years of searching, we’re proud to begin this new chapter in our shop in Middletown—a major milestone for our team. As we continue to grow, our vision is to train skilled professionals, develop future leaders, and expand into multiple locations while maintaining the same level of quality and care that defines our name.

At Auto Recon, we don’t just clean cars—we transform them. We restore vehicles to like-new condition, protect them with durable solutions ranging from 1 to 10 years, and preserve that finish with professional-grade maintenance systems.
